Good to know

About the prints

Are the prints limited editions?

Both are available. Each limited edition is restricted to 3 to 5 prints of a photograph, all in the same size. Limited-edition prints are signed and numbered, come with a certificate of authenticity, and are shipped by me. Open-edition prints are also available.

What sizes are available?

Each limited edition is printed in one size only, chosen to suit the photograph: 30 W × 40 H cm, 80 W × 60 H cm or 90 W × 60 H cm. Open-edition prints on Photo4me come in various other formats.

How much does a print cost?

Limited-edition prints start from €350, for a 30 W × 40 H cm print on fine art paper. The price depends on the photograph, its size and the material, and it rises as an edition sells. Open-edition prices are shown on each gallery.

What paper and materials are used?

Prints are made on museum-grade, 350 g/m² fine art paper or on 400 g/m² linen canvas for exceptional quality and longevity.

Where can I buy a print?
  • Saatchi Art: limited-edition originals shipped by me, and open-edition prints printed and shipped by Saatchi Art.
  • Artfinder: limited editions shipped by me.
  • Photo4me: open-edition prints in various formats, printed and shipped by Photo4me.

Which gallery should I choose?

Prices and shipping options vary across galleries, so compare them for the print you want and choose the one that suits you. For a signed, numbered limited edition, look at Saatchi Art or Artfinder; for an open-edition print, look at Saatchi Art or Photo4me.

Can I buy a print directly from you?

Yes, for limited-edition prints only. Send an enquiry through the contact page. Where import and export rules allow, I can ship the print to you directly. Direct sales are final and non-returnable, and orders are shipped once full payment has been received.

How can I check that a print is genuine?

Every limited-edition print comes with a certificate of authenticity. Scan its QR code, or enter the certificate number on the Verify a certificate page.
